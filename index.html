The FAQ

Everything you need to know before ordering The Cold Weather Capsule.

180 looks for virtually any occasion, built from 15 foundational garments — 3 top layers, 4 mid layers, 5 base layers, and 3 pairs of trousers — that all work together. Everything arrives in a single delivery. Pieces are sourced from a curated mix of quality menswear brands (we deliberately avoid fast fashion), so the exact brand behind any given garment may vary slightly capsule to capsule depending on sizing and availability at the time of your order — the fabric, colour, and fit specification always stay consistent.

The math is exact: 3 top layers × 4 mid layers × 5 base layers × 3 trousers = 180 combinations. Every pairing is intentional, not incidental. The point isn't 180 wildly different outfits — it's versatility. Every one of them looks sharp and suits the moment, from a morning meeting to a late dinner, so you never have to think twice about what to wear. Each piece was designed as part of one coordinated colour and fabric system — a warm neutral palette of charcoal, black, navy, grey, brown, white, light blue, and moss green — so any top layer sits comfortably over any mid layer, over any base layer, above any pair of trousers. That's why there are no "wrong" combinations to avoid. It also means the capsule flexes with the weather: on the coldest days, wear all four layers together; on a milder or heated-indoor day, shed the outer layer and the mid layer becomes your top. Try the interactive lookbook above to click through all 180 looks yourself.

No in-person fitting required. At checkout, you'll provide a few simple measurement inputs — height, weight, and age — which is typically all that's needed to size menswear accurately. We also build the capsule toward a slightly more relaxed fit overall, which is both a common preference and more forgiving across a range of body types than a closely tailored fit would be.

Reach out to our team once your capsule has arrived and you've had a chance to try everything on — we're happy to arrange a full return of the whole package. Because the capsule is designed to work together as one interlocking system, a piecemeal exchange of just one piece can be difficult to accommodate, but we'll always do our best to make it right for you.

Cashmere, mohair-blend wool, and corduroy each have their own care requirements. Every piece ships with its care label sewn in, and a full garment-by-garment care guide is included with your capsule.
