The FAQ

Everything you need to know before ordering The Cold Weather Capsule.

180 looks for virtually any occasion, built from 15 foundational garments — 3 outerwear pieces, 4 mid layers, 5 base layers, and 3 pairs of pants — that all work together. Everything arrives in a single delivery. Pieces are sourced from a curated mix of quality menswear brands (we deliberately avoid fast fashion), so the exact brand behind any given garment may vary slightly capsule to capsule depending on sizing and availability at the time of your order.

The math is exact: 3 outerwear × 4 mid layers × 5 base layers × 3 pants = 180 combinations. Every pairing is intentional, not incidental. The point isn't 180 wildly different outfits — it's versatility. Every one of them looks sharp and suits the moment, from a morning meeting to a late dinner, so you never have to think twice about what to wear. Each piece was designed as part of one coordinated colour and fabric system — a warm neutral palette of charcoal, black, navy, grey, brown, white, light blue, and moss green — so any outerwear piece sits comfortably over any mid layer, over any base layer, above any pair of pants. That's why there are no "wrong" combinations to avoid. It also means the capsule flexes with the weather: on the coldest days, wear all four layers together; on a milder or heated-indoor day, shed the outerwear and the mid layer becomes your top. Try the interactive lookbook above to click through all 180 looks yourself.

Winter is coming, and the first cold morning is when you find out what's worn out, out of date, or no longer fits. Get it sorted before then, and before the run of year-end events where you want to look good. And if you're starting a new job or heading back to the office, this is the moment to do it once and be finished.

It's the smallest wardrobe that covers everything. Five base layers, one for each workday. Four mid layers for added variety. Three each of outerwear and pants, to range from warm to light and from more formal to more casual. Enough for a different look every day of winter.

Two good suits cost about the same and only give you a handful of looks. This gives you 180, at about $22 a look, and it's more versatile, more comfortable, and more current than the suit. The styling is already done: every piece was chosen to work with every other. And it arrives in one delivery, which saves you the shopping trips and the packaging and shipping of a dozen separate orders.

No in-person fitting required. At checkout, you'll provide a few simple measurement inputs — height, weight, and age — which is typically all that's needed to size menswear accurately. We also build the capsule toward a slightly more relaxed fit overall, which is both a common preference and more forgiving across a range of body types than a closely tailored fit would be.
